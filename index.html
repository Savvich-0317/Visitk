Secure, encrypted peer-to-peer chatting — no accounts, no registrations, just one powerful application.

TcpChat delivers reliable two-way encryption for safe communication, while also allowing unencrypted connections when you need simplicity. Choose between a clean command-line interface or an upcoming text-based UI (TUI still wip tho).

Built entirely on peer-to-peer connections, TcpChat never requires accounts or sign-ups. Communication happens directly between users using IP addresses — fast, private, and fully under your control.

Its open source! Feel free to explore the code, contribute improvements, customize it to your needs, or even build your own version from scratch. Actually i think its possible to compile for basically any os, linux is only tested for now.

Setting up

First of all you need an open port for p2p communication, it may be open by your internet provider, or may be charged for extra pay. check port

After port is opened, download TcpChat, set easy toml config to your needs, generate keys and your done! Basically in around 50 key presses!
